Operational Interpretation & Synthesis

SNUNet-ECAM deep Siamese segmentation identifies 2.87 hectares of filtered standing flood water (41 polygons ≥ 500 m²; 15.76 ha raw raster) along river terraces. Meanwhile, classical radar drop (ΔVV < -3 dB, slope ≤ 15°) reveals 138.10 hectares of physical surface alteration and debris scouring. This comparison is strictly an independent cross-hazard spatial overlap analysis, not flood ground truth.
